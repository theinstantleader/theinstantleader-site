Sound familiar?

The business is doing well. People are buying. But more of it runs through you than you'd like.

  • Decisions wait for you, even the small ones.
  • You know the team is busy. You're less sure what they're working on.
  • Leads are coming in, but sales feel harder than they should.
  • The numbers are in there somewhere. You just don't fully trust them.
  • Clients ask for a little bit more each week, and the team finds it hard to say no.
  • You hired someone good, and it still hasn't quite landed.
  • Thinking time happens in the evenings, if it happens at all.

That's normal at this stage. The business grew, and the way it runs didn't get the chance to catch up. It's usually fixable. And it's often fewer things than it feels like.

What changes

I work beside you as an experienced operator. We find the few things holding the business back. Then I help you and the team fix them, one at a time.

What we work on depends on what the Founder Review finds. It's often things like:

  • Getting decisions off your desk and onto the right person's, with clear limits.
  • Making your offer and sales process easier to say yes to.
  • A simple weekly meeting rhythm, and one place where the work is tracked.
  • A short dashboard with the numbers you actually check, so you're not digging for them.
  • A standard way to onboard new clients, so scope is clear from day one.
  • Help with the hard conversations, including what to say.

Where it's quicker, I'll draft the process, the email or the brief myself. I won't run your team day to day. That stays with you and the people you've hired.

Over time, fewer things need you. That's the goal.

How the first 30 days work

Every client starts the same way.

Week 1

We have a 90-minute session on where the business is and where it's getting stuck. You give me read access to your team's tools and your numbers, and fill in a short intake form.

Week 2

I have short conversations with 2 or 3 people on your team, about 20 minutes each. I sit in on one team meeting and look at your offer and sales process.

Week 3

We have a 60-minute playback. You get the Founder Review: the top 3 things holding the business back, the options for each and what I'd recommend. Plus a 90-day plan with owners and dates.

Week 4

The first changes go in. Usually that's who owns which decisions, the meeting rhythm, one place to track the work and a first draft of your dashboard.

Weeks 1 to 3 are the Founder Review. You can stop there and keep the plan. If you'd like me to help you carry it out, we carry on into the monthly support.

Who it's for

This is for you if

  • You run an established service, agency, software or online business, with roughly 3 to 30 people on the team.
  • The business already sells, and more of it runs through you than you'd like.
  • You're happy to give me access to the team and the numbers.
  • You're ready to make decisions and see them through.

Who it's not for

  • If you're still at the idea stage, or not yet making sales, this is too early.
  • If you need a full-time operations manager, I'm not that. The Founder Review will tell you if that's what you need.
  • If you're looking for guaranteed revenue numbers, I can't promise those. Nobody can.
  • If you need someone on US hours, I'm not the right fit.
  • If you compete with a client I already work with, I'll tell you on the call.

Questions

Have you built a business like mine yourself?

No. I haven't founded and scaled a company like yours, and I won't pretend I have. I've spent most of my 20+ years working inside businesses, often right beside the founder, on the operating side. That's the part that turns your ideas into a team that delivers without everything coming back to you. It's also why we start with the Founder Review. You'll see whether I can find what's holding you back before you commit to anything longer.

How much of my time does it take?

The weekly call is 45 minutes. It has an agenda, so it stays 45 minutes. There's a 30-minute review once a month, and messages fit around your week. The Founder Review asks a bit more at the start. There's a 90-minute first session and a 60-minute playback, plus a short form to fill in. Your team gives about 20 minutes each.

When do you reply to messages?

Monday to Thursday, between 10am and 4pm UK. I reply within one working day. Send things whenever suits you. If you message on a Thursday evening, you'll hear back on Monday. I don't work evenings or weekends, on purpose. It's a rhythm I'll probably be suggesting to you too.

What if something's urgent?

Put "urgent" at the top of your message and I'll look at it first in my next working window. Urgent means money or a client relationship is at risk today.

What notice do I need to give?

There's a 3-month minimum, so there's time to put the plan in place. After that it's month to month, with 30 days' notice.

What if the Founder Review shows I don't need you?

Then I'll tell you. You keep the report and the 90-day plan either way.

Is this the same as a fractional COO?

It's close. A fractional COO usually works set days each week inside your business. I work beside you, mostly by message, with a weekly call and a couple of team meetings a month.

Is it all online?

Yes. Calls are on Zoom, so you can join from wherever you are.
