About The Instant Leader

What is The Instant Leader?

It's where I help people grow into the responsibility life has handed them. That might be a team at work, your own business, a role at church or things at home. You get practical tools you can use the same week, and someone to talk it through with.

Who is this for?

Anyone who's been handed more responsibility, at work or anywhere else in life.

Do I need a management title?

No. You don't need a title to lead. Some of the people I talk to have a big one. Lots don't. They're just the person others look to. If people are looking to you, that's enough to start.

Is this faith-based?

Faith shapes how I treat people. Everything here is open to you, whatever you believe.

Is it right for me?

I run a business. Is this for me?

Yes. It's really common for a business to grow faster than the way it's run. Then more and more of it lands on your desk. We'd look at what can move to your team, and how to hand it over so it stays handed over. For established founders, I also work 1:1 as an experienced operator beside you. It's called Operator in Your Corner, and we can see on the call whether it fits.

Does it help outside work, like church, community or home?

Yes. Leading volunteers is its own thing. People give their time freely, so a job title won't carry you. At home, a lot of the same tools help. Listening properly, for example, or handling a hard conversation calmly. We can look at your work and your home life together.

Working with Andy

How do I work with you?

Start with a free 20-minute call. If it makes sense to work together, I'll suggest what I think would help most. If it doesn't, I'll tell you. Either way, you'll know your next step.

What happens on the call?

We talk about what's changed for you and what's getting in the way. You'll leave with a clear next step, even if it isn't working with me.

What happens after the call?

Nothing, unless you want it to. If there's a fit, I'll send you a short note with what I'd suggest and what it costs. You decide in your own time.

Is it one-to-one or in a group?

Mostly one-to-one, so it's built around what you're facing. We'll work out what suits you on the call.

What tools do you use?

Simple frameworks I've built from 20+ years of experience. For example, the 3 Gears of Management help you notice when you're still doing the work yourself, and when it's time to step back. The C.A.R.E. Conversation Model gives you a structure for the conversation you've been putting off. There are a few more. We only use the ones that help with what's in front of you.

How quickly will I notice a difference?

It depends on you and what's going on, so I can't promise a timescale. What we work on is small and practical. You can usually try it the same week.
